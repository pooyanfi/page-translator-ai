Why the new privacy policy matters

The company says the new policy makes data handling clearer, but critics disagree.

The new privacy policy, which was announced on Monday, will apply to all users and it takes effect immediately.

If you do not agree with these terms, you can opt out by contacting support before the end of the month.

Under the old rules, the company could share anonymised data with partners without asking. The new text says it will only do so when you turn the option on.

What changes for you

  • Read the full document carefully.
  • Check your email for confirmation.
  • Review the privacy settings on every device.
“We want people to understand exactly what we collect and why,” a spokesperson said.
